# Bundled offline lecture and assistant voices

Runtime: sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 (Apache 2.0), including ONNX Runtime (MIT).
Source: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.13.8
ONNX Runtime source: https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

Lecture models: Piper en_US-ljspeech-medium-int8 and sq_AL-arta-medium.
Assistant models: Piper en_US-norman-medium-int8 and sq_AL-edon-medium-int8.
The Piper voice repository is published with the MIT license tag:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices
Original model cards are included here as english-MODEL_CARD,
english-male-MODEL_CARD, albanian-MODEL_CARD and albanian-arta-MODEL_CARD.
LJ Speech training data: public domain.
Norman training data: LibriVox, public domain; trained from scratch.
Norman model card:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/c10ece1aade47bb51c153c893d14e5bf8e5b7117/en/en_US/norman/medium/MODEL_CARD
Edon training data: CC0; fine-tuned from Lessac medium as recorded in the model card.

Arta's model and configuration are licensed separately under AGPL-3.0
(see albanian-arta-MODEL_CARD and arta-AGPL-3.0).
Corresponding publisher model/configuration:
https://huggingface.co/edonseki/folsh.ai/tree/96a0ec37123333cb36fec91c5ce1b769081cfca6/arta
The bundled Arta weights are unchanged. We add sherpa-onnx ONNX metadata
and derive tokens from the original configuration. Conversion source:
scripts/convert-piper-model.ps1 in the application's source distribution.
Preserve these notices and the corresponding source required by AGPL-3.0
when redistributing the model and its conversion.

Phonemizer: eSpeak NG, GPL 3.0 or later (see espeak-ng-COPYING).
Source: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
Sherpa's integration and pinned native build dependencies:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.13.8/cmake

The phonemizer converts text to pronunciation symbols. The audible speech is
generated by the bundled neural models, not the eSpeak synthesized voice.
